ChordCraft - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

ChordCraft is an Android guitar chord learning application developed and published by CircleCraft Apps.

1. Personal Information

ChordCraft does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or other personal information to use the application.

ChordCraft does not incorporate advertising networks, third-party analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

2. Application Data

Application preferences and settings may be stored locally on the user's device to support normal application functionality.

ChordCraft does not independently transmit these locally stored preferences to the developer's servers.

3. Google Play Purchases

ChordCraft offers an optional Pro upgrade through Google Play Billing.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. ChordCraft may receive purchase-related information, such as product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens, to verify and restore Pro access.

CircleCraft Apps does not receive or store users' credit card numbers or payment card details.

Google's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Advertising and Analytics

ChordCraft does not display advertisements and does not integrate advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services.

5. Data Sharing

CircleCraft Apps does not sell users' personal information.

Purchase-related information is processed through Google Play as necessary to provide the optional Pro upgrade.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application data can generally be removed by clearing ChordCraft's application storage or uninstalling the application, subject to Android device backup settings.

Google Play purchase records are managed by Google under its applicable policies and retention practices.

7. Children's Privacy

ChordCraft is designed as a general-purpose music education application and is not specifically directed at children.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the application.

8. Security

ChordCraft uses Google Play's billing infrastructure for purchases. The developer takes reasonable measures to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when ChordCraft's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning ChordCraft, please contact CircleCraft Apps through the developer contact details provided on its Google Play Store listing.
